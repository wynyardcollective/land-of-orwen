The Land of Orwen is an original idle RPG set in a drought-struck countryside. Travel the map in real time, pursue quests, gather materials, craft gear, visit taverns, and work toward the Rainward Gate.

This Android app is the official mobile client for rough.co.nz. Gameplay runs online — an internet connection is required. You can play as a guest (progress on this device) or create an account to sync saves across devices.

Features:
• Interactive map with towns, secrets, and skill locations
• Fishing, mining, woodcutting, cooking, and smithing
• Shops, taverns, equipment, and gem crafting
• Accessibility options including font scale and high contrast
